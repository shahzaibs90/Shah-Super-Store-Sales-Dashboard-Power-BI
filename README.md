# Super Store Sales Dashboard | Microsoft Power BI

## Project Overview
The Super Store Sales Dashboard is an interactive business intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI. It provides insights into sales performance, profitability, order quantities, delivery metrics, and sales trends to support data-driven business decisions.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Total Sales
- Average Delivery Time
- Total Profit
- Total Quantity Sold

## Dashboard Features

### 1. Sales Forecasting
- Forecast of sales for the next 15 days to support short-term planning.

### 2. Geographic Analysis
- Sales and profit by state using map visualizations.
- State-level sales comparison.

### 3. Year-over-Year Trend Analysis
- Monthly profit comparison by year using a stacked area chart.
- Monthly sales comparison by year using a stacked area chart.

### 4. Sales Analysis by Payment Mode
- Donut chart showing the distribution of sales by payment mode:
  - Card
  - Cash on Delivery (COD)
  - Online

### 5. Sales Analysis by Customer Segment
- Donut chart showing sales contribution by:
  - Consumer
  - Corporate
  - Home Office

### 6. Category and Business Performance
- Clustered bar charts for sales by category and other business dimensions.
- Comparative analysis of sales across states and product groups.

### 7. Interactive Slicers
- Sales
- Average Delivery
- Profit
- Quantity

## Tools and Technologies
- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
- Data Modeling
- Data Visualization
- Sales Forecasting
- Business Intelligence and Reporting

## Business Value
This dashboard helps users monitor key performance indicators, identify sales and profitability trends, compare geographic performance, understand customer purchasing patterns, and explore short-term sales forecasts.
